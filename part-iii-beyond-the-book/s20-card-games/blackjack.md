## Blackjack

This step runs [`blackjack.py`](./blackjack.py). It uses the card classes from
the earlier lessons (`Deck`, `Hand`, and `Ranks`) to play a full game of
blackjack.

The rules and the code are explained step by step in
[Playing Blackjack](./03_blackjack.md). Read that lesson first if you want to
see how the game works.

---

### Run the Game

Press **Run** to play in this window. You start with $5,000 and place a bet
on each hand. Enter `q` at the bet prompt to quit.

Each hand pauses at the points where the game waits for a choice.

---

### Try It

Play a few hands and try each option:

* **(H)it** takes another card.
* **(S)tand** keeps your hand and ends your turn.
* **(D)ouble down** appears on your first two cards. It doubles your bet and
  gives you exactly one more card.

Play until you have a blackjack, a push, and a bust, so you see each result.
