# Chapter 13: Try It Yourself

These exercises build on the chapter's ideas with small programs of their own. Each exercise is in
its own folder, and runs by itself, separate from the Alien Invasion steps.

| Folder | Exercise | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `13-1-stars` | 13-1: a grid of stars on the screen | Complete |
| `13-2-better-stars` | 13-2: stars at random positions | Complete |
| `13-3-raindrops` | 13-3: raindrops that fall until they disappear | Complete |
| `13-4-steady-rain` | 13-4: rain that keeps falling, with new drops at the top | Complete |
| `13-5-sideways-shooter-2` | 13-5: a sideways shooter with aliens | Complete |
| `13-6-game-over` | 13-6: a sideways shooter that ends the game | Complete |

To run an exercise, open a terminal in its folder, and run the program named on its lesson page
with `python`.

## Images

Each exercise has its own `images` folder. The star images are in `13-1-stars` and
`13-2-better-stars`, the raindrop images are in `13-3-raindrops` and `13-4-steady-rain`, and the
rocket image is in `13-5-sideways-shooter-2` and `13-6-game-over`.
